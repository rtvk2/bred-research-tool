Informed consent

Title of study: Recognizing Unfamiliar Words: A Memory Study

You are invited to take part in this research study. This page explains what taking part involves so that you can decide whether you want to. Please read it carefully. You must be 18 or older to take part.

Purpose of this study

To investigate how people recognize English words that they have studied, and how information about other people's responses can affect these judgments.

Some details of the study cannot be given to you now, because knowing them in advance could change how you respond. They will be fully explained to you at the end.

What will happen

The study is completed online, in one sitting, on a laptop, desktop computer or phone. It has these steps:

  1. A short practice.
  2. Learning: you will see a list of 25 words, one at a time, for 5 seconds each (about 2 minutes). Some are real English words and some are made-up words that look like English words. Try to remember them exactly as they appear.
  3. A one-minute shape task.
  4. Recognition: you will see 60 words, one at a time. For each word, indicate whether it was in the list you studied (OLD) or not (NEW). For most words you will also see information about how other participants responded. Now and then a short question about that information will appear.
  5. A few short questions about the study, about the languages you use, and about you (age, gender, programme). You will not be asked for your name, email address or roll number.

At the end you will be told more about the study, and you can ask questions.

Who can take part

You can take part if you are 18 or older, can read English comfortably, and are using a laptop, desktop computer or phone. You can take part only once.

Expected duration of overall participation

About 15 minutes, in a single session.

Benefits expected from this research

The study will add to our understanding of how memory is influenced by social information. There is no direct benefit to you.

Will I be made aware of the results?

If you wish to have the results of the study forwarded to you, please email the study team (details below) and we shall forward the results to you when they are published.

Potential risks to the participant from this research

There are no serious risks to your health from this study. You may find the tasks mildly tiring, or feel unsure about your own memory. If you feel uncomfortable at any point, you can stop (see below).

Confidentiality of records

We do not collect your name, email address or roll number. Your answers are recorded under a random code, not under your name. We also record basic technical information (your browser, operating system, screen size and time zone). If you stop before the end, we keep only a note of how far you got, without any of your answers. Your IP address is not recorded by the study software; the websites that host the study may keep their own standard technical logs.

Your answers are stored in an account or system controlled by the research team and can be accessed only by the research team. They are kept and disposed of according to the research data guidelines of IIIT-Hyderabad.

The information from this study, if published in scientific journals or presented at scientific meetings, will not reveal your identity. By agreeing below, you allow the research team to view your data if required.

Use of data generated

The data and results from this study will be used only for scientific purposes. Under the consent box you can choose whether your anonymous answers may also be uploaded to a public research database, so that other researchers can use them. This choice is optional and does not affect whether you can take part.

Freedom to withdraw from the study

Taking part is purely voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason and without any penalty, by clicking Quit study (top right of every screen) or by closing the page. If you stop before the end, none of your answers are saved.

When you finish, you will be shown a participant code. Your answers are anonymous, so we can remove them later only if you send us that code; please keep it if you might want that. Without the code we cannot tell which answers are yours.
