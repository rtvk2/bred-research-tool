Replace this whole file with the text of your own, IRB-approved consent form. It is deliberately left for the researchers to write. While the marker comment at the top is present, the app refuses to run real participants; use ?dev=1 to test.

The wording must match your consent and IRB forms. Elements to cover (from the course notes):

  • Who is running the study (names, roll numbers, institute), who reviewed it, how to contact you.
  • Purpose, described with partial disclosure: say that some information is withheld until the end.
  • What happens: one online session of about 10 to 12 minutes, made-up words, an old/new test, a few questions.
  • Voluntary; can quit at any time before submitting (the Quit link: nothing is saved); anonymous data cannot be removed after submission unless the participant keeps the code shown at the end.
  • Risks: write "no anticipated risks", not "no risks". Benefits. Compensation (or none).
  • Confidentiality: no names, emails or roll numbers collected; where data are stored; for how long; whether anonymised data go on OSF.
  • Adults only (18+).
